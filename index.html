    Help & support
    How do levels work?

    Your level moves through the standard A1 to C2 steps. Every finished session adds XP. When your recent scores are high, the app suggests moving up.

    How do I unlock more scenarios?

    Invite friends from your Profile. Every friend who signs up with your code unlocks 2 more scenarios and 2 more conversations.

    The microphone is not working

    Allow microphone access for this site in your browser settings, use Chrome or Safari, and check your internet connection. You can always type your answer instead.

    Is my data safe?

    In this prototype, your account and progress are saved only on this device. The full app will store them securely on a server.

    How do I delete my account?

    Open Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This erases your data on this device.
